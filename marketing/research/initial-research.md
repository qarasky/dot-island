# Initial research / superseded direction

Researched 2026-10-11. Preserved as a record of the first campaign, not guidance
to restore the approximate UI. The complete original brief lives in commit
`8cc23a1` at `marketing/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`.

## Original brief

A joke launch for a real personal Android app. Audience: design-minded Android
tinkerers who appreciate obsessive details and over-serious product launches.
Make the ring memorable, demonstrate why it becomes useful, and land the joke
that the engineering serves one phone. Not a public commercial release.

The initial design used off-white technical paper, monochrome surfaces, hairline
rules, dimension marks, Barlow/IBM Plex Mono typography, an SVG island demonstration
and a deadpan acquisition panel. Those technical labels and approximate drawings
were rejected in review. The soundtrack, 32-second edit rhythm and campaign name
were retained.

## Original reference study

These are craft references, not proof of conversion. Award scores are aesthetic
judgements. No conversion rate was inferred.

- [FFrames official skill](https://github.com/dmtrKovalenko/fframes/tree/main/skills/fframes-video)
  and design/API/audio guides: readable holds, safe margins, bundled fonts,
  deterministic motion, contact-sheet inspection, audio peak verification.
- [Remotion official agent skills](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills) and
  [repository](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills): React UI reuse, frame-based
  animation and composition previews. FFrames chosen for SVG geometry/typography
  with locally available Metal. Revision 02 also uses its bundled-media pipeline.
- [Google ABCDs](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en): hook
  immediately, brand early, focus one message, connect through humor, finish with
  a specific action. Use readable visual storytelling instead of a stock narrator.
- [Opal Tadpole / Awwwards SOTD](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/opal-tadpole)
  (2024, 7.52): object-led storytelling, macro detail, small-product confidence.
- [Opal Camera / Awwwards SOTD](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/opal-camera-inc)
  (2023, 7.42) and [current Opal](https://op.al/): tactile product world and editorial
  voice. Current site differs from the archived winner; not conflated.
- [Nothing ecommerce reference](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/websites/nothing):
  technical labels and monochrome hierarchy, without copying its identity or dot
  wordmark. This influence became too decorative in the first campaign.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/): product evidence near the promise and repeated
  clear action. Avoid its now-common glow/bento vocabulary.
- [Copywriting skill](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/copywriting):
  specific benefits, one argument per section, honest proof, objection handling,
  concrete CTAs. Avoid “reimagined”, “unlock”, fake urgency and inflated features.

## Original film structure

0–4: Much ado about a dot. 4–9: music. 9–14: pizza delivery. 14–20: hold/release.
20–26: built for one phone. 26–32: Dot Island and source CTA.

Revision 02 keeps the timing but replaces the uncaptured delivery drawing with
the actual home-screen music context, and replaces every UI drawing with captured
pixels. No fake reviews, performance measurements or source ETA were introduced.
